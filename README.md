# 🎬 Film_ACT

### Connecting Local Actors with Filmmakers

Film_ACT is an online collaboration platform designed to connect actors and filmmakers, making it easier for filmmakers to discover local acting talent and for actors to find opportunities within the film industry.

Think of Film_ACT as **LinkedIn for actors and filmmakers** — a dedicated professional networking space where creative professionals can showcase their talents, connect with one another, and collaborate on film projects.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Finding the right cast is one of the challenges local filmmakers face when producing films. At the same time, many talented actors struggle to find opportunities to showcase their abilities and connect with filmmakers.

Film_ACT aims to bridge this gap by providing a centralized platform where:

- Actors can create professional profiles and showcase their acting abilities.
- Filmmakers can discover and connect with local acting talents.
- Creative professionals can build networks within the film industry.
- Actors and filmmakers can find opportunities to collaborate on film projects.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of Film_ACT are to:

1. Make it easier for local filmmakers to find suitable actors for their productions.
2. Give upcoming and established actors a platform to showcase their talents.
3. Encourage collaboration within local film communities.
4. Build connections between actors, directors, producers, and other filmmaking professionals.
5. Promote the discovery of local talent and support the growth of the local film industry.

## ✨ Key Features (Planned)

- **User Registration and Authentication:** Allow actors and filmmakers to create accounts and securely access the platform.
- **Professional Profiles:** Enable users to create profiles containing relevant information about their experience, skills, and interests.
- **Talent Discovery:** Allow filmmakers to browse and discover actors based on their profiles and abilities.
- **Networking:** Help actors and filmmakers connect and establish professional relationships.
- **Collaboration Opportunities:** Provide a space for creative professionals to discover potential film projects and collaborators.

*Note: These features describe the intended functionality of the platform. Their implementation status depends on the current development progress.*

## 🛠️ Technologies and Project Management Tools

- **Git:** Version control and tracking changes to project files.
- **GitHub:** Hosting the source-code repository and supporting collaborative development.
- **Jira:** Integration for Software Configuration Management (SCM), including organizing and tracking project work, change requests, tasks, and their status.

Jira will be used alongside GitHub to support the team's configuration management workflow. Project changes and work items can be tracked in Jira, while source-code changes and version history are managed through Git and GitHub.

## 🔄 Software Configuration Management

The team will use configuration management practices to keep project files organized, track changes, and maintain consistency throughout development.

The SCM workflow will use:

1. **Git and GitHub** to manage source-code versions, branches, commits, and code reviews.
2. **Jira** to record and track tasks, change requests, assigned work, and progress.
3. **Team reviews and approvals** to help ensure that changes are checked before they are incorporated into the main project version.
4. **Project documentation** to record important configuration decisions and development activities.

## 🚀 Project Vision

Our vision is to make discovering acting talent and building filmmaking connections easier, more accessible, and more collaborative.

We believe that talented actors should have opportunities to be discovered, regardless of their location or level of experience, and that filmmakers should have easier access to the creative talent within their communities.
